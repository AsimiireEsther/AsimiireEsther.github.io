Research & presentations

Research paper · SSRN · August 2026 · 17 pages

Predictive Analysis of Alternating Current (AC) Power Output Using Environmental Variables with Artificial Intelligence ↗

Hwindingwi, T., Mubayiwa, B., Bagombeka, E., & Ngwarati, F.

The study compares four machine learning models (linear, polynomial and spline regression, and K-nearest neighbors) for predicting the AC power output of solar PV inverters. The models use irradiation, ambient and module temperature, and time of day. The data is 68,978 observations recorded at 15-minute intervals from a real solar installation.
